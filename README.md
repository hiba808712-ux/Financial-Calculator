# Financial-Calculator
Built a financial calculator using excel and python. Made it customizable so users can enter their own invested amount and rate of return, a series of cash flows will be generated which will give them a rough idea whether an investment is viable or not. Furthermore, initiated three different scenarios namely bull, base and bear with varying economic conditions aka varying interest rates which gives users an idea of what will be the financial health outlook of their investments in varying economic conditions and uncertainties.
